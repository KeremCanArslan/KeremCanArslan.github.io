Mobile app

Portly: stock market and portfolio tracker

Mobile and web app · Flutter · FastAPI · PostgreSQL

Graduation project, lead developer. Turkish (BIST) and US stocks, crypto, currencies, investment funds and physical gold are tracked in one portfolio. Every stock gets a BUY, HOLD or SELL signal from technical indicators and company ratios, with the reasoning shown next to it. News is scored as positive or negative with a Turkish financial lexicon.

  • Live prices, intraday and long-term charts, drawing tools
  • Signals with reasons from RSI, MACD and moving averages
  • News score and economic calendar: central bank rate decisions, US data
  • Portfolio health score, sector and currency breakdown
  • Price alerts, transaction history, import from broker statements
