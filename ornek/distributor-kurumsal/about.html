About us

Built on reliability since 2008

Meridia Trade is a privately owned distribution company headquartered in Istanbul, with operations in Romania and the UAE.

Who we are

From importer to full-service distributor

We started in 2008 with a single confectionery line and one rented warehouse. Today we represent more than 300 brands, operate 45,000 m² of storage and deliver to retail chains, wholesalers and HoReCa customers in 42 countries.

Our model is simple: exclusive long-term agreements with manufacturers, our own logistics, and sales teams that know every shelf in their region.

  • Own fleet of 24 vehicles
  • Bonded warehouse in Mersin port
  • ISO 9001 and IFS Logistics certified
  • Chilled and ambient lines
  • Sales teams in 3 countries
  • EDI integration with major retailers
Milestones

Eighteen years of growth
