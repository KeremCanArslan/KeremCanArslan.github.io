About us

A distribution partner, not just a supplier

Founded in 2008 in Istanbul, Meridia Trade grew from a regional confectionery importer into a full-service distributor with its own fleet, bonded warehouses and sales teams in three countries.

We handle import, customs, labeling, storage and last-mile delivery, so manufacturers can focus on their product and retailers on their shelves.
